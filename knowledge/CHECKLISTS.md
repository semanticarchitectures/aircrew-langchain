# CONDOR OPERATIONS — STANDARD CHECKLISTS
**Document:** CHECKLISTS
**Aircraft Type:** CONDOR-class Heavy Cargo (Fictional)
**Primary User:** Co-Pilot (calls out items); AC confirms unless item is assigned to another crew member
**Classification:** DEMONSTRATION — UNCLASSIFIED FICTIONAL

---

> **CHECKLIST PROTOCOL:**
> Co-Pilot calls each item aloud. The responsible crew member responds with the required state.
> Co-Pilot proceeds only when a response is received. AC has authority to pause or restart any checklist.
> Emergency checklists (marked ⚠) take priority over all standard checklists.

---
