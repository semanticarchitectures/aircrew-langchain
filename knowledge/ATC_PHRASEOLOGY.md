# ALPHA APPROACH — ATC PHRASEOLOGY AND COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
**Document:** ATC_PHRASEOLOGY
**Primary Users:** Co-Pilot (COPILOT) and ATC agent
**Classification:** DEMONSTRATION — UNCLASSIFIED FICTIONAL

---

## 1. PRINCIPLES

All ATC communications follow three rules:
1. **Brevity** — say what is needed and nothing more
2. **Accuracy** — readbacks must be exact; never paraphrase clearances
3. **Clarity** — numbers spoken digit-by-digit; standard phrases used consistently

Both the Co-Pilot and ATC controller use this document as the reference for correct phrasing.

---

## 2. NUMBER AND ALTITUDE CONVENTIONS

| Item | Convention | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Flight levels | "Flight level [digits]" | FL180 → "flight level one-eight-zero" |
| Altitudes | "[Digits] feet" | 2,500 ft → "two thousand five hundred feet" |
| Headings | "[Three digits]" | 285° → "heading two-eight-five" |
| Frequencies | Each digit, decimal spoken | 124.35 → "one-two-four-point-three-five" |
| Squawk codes | Four digits | 4712 → "squawk four-seven-one-two" |
| Speeds | "[Digits] knots" | 150 kt → "one-five-zero knots" |
| Time | Zulu (UTC), four digits | 1430Z → "one-four-three-zero Zulu" |

## 3. STANDARD EXCHANGE LIBRARY

### 3.1 Departure Clearance
**ATC issues:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Cleared to Iron Mountain via filed route. Maintain flight level one-eight-zero. Squawk four-seven-one-two. Runway two-seven-left, wind two-five-zero at one-two."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Cleared to Iron Mountain via filed route, maintain flight level one-eight-zero, squawk four-seven-one-two, runway two-seven-left. CONDOR 71."

**ATC confirms:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Readback correct. Cleared for takeoff when ready."

---

### 3.2 Altitude Change (ATC-initiated)
**ATC issues:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Descend and maintain flight level one-four-zero. Pilot reports of moderate turbulence flight level one-six-zero through two-zero-zero."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Descend and maintain flight level one-four-zero. CONDOR 71."

**ATC confirms:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Readback correct."

---

### 3.3 Altitude Change (Pilot-requested)
**Co-Pilot requests:**
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Request descent to flight level one-four-zero. We have pilot reports of turbulence at current altitude."

**ATC approves:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Approved. Descend and maintain flight level one-four-zero."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Descend and maintain flight level one-four-zero. CONDOR 71."

---

### 3.4 Romeo-4 Transit Request
**Co-Pilot requests:**
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Requesting transit through Restricted Area Romeo-4. Cargo airdrop mission. Estimated entry one-four-one-five Zulu, exit one-four-three-zero Zulu."

**ATC approves:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Cleared through Restricted Area Romeo-4. Maintain flight level one-four-zero or below. Report clear of Romeo-4."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Cleared through Romeo-4, maintain flight level one-four-zero or below, will report clear. CONDOR 71."

**ATC denies (traffic conflict):**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Unable Romeo-4 transit at this time. Traffic conflict. Expect clearance at one-four-two-five Zulu. Advise ready."

---

### 3.5 Holding Instruction
**ATC issues hold:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Hold north of Waypoint Alpha on the two-eight-five-degree radial. Right turns. Leg time two minutes. Expect further clearance one-four-three-zero Zulu."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Hold north of Waypoint Alpha, two-eight-five-degree radial, right turns, two-minute legs. Expect clearance one-four-three-zero Zulu. CONDOR 71."

---

### 3.6 Position Report
**Co-Pilot reports:**
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Passing Waypoint Bravo, flight level one-eight-zero. Estimating Drop Zone Alpha at one-four-two-zero Zulu."

**ATC acknowledges:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Roger. Report Romeo-4 entry."

---

### 3.7 Weather Advisory (ATC-initiated PIREP)
**ATC issues:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Pilot report — moderate turbulence between flight level one-six-zero and two-zero-zero near Waypoint Bravo. Pilots requesting lower altitude reporting improvement at flight level one-four-zero."

**Co-Pilot acknowledges:**
> "Copy turbulence flight level one-six-zero through two-zero-zero near Bravo. CONDOR 71."

*(Co-Pilot then relays to AC before taking any action.)*

---

### 3.8 Traffic Advisory
**ATC issues:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Traffic advisory. Cargo aircraft, eleven o'clock, ten miles, flight level one-eight-zero, eastbound. Report traffic in sight."

**Co-Pilot responds — traffic in sight:**
> "Traffic in sight. CONDOR 71."

**Co-Pilot responds — traffic not in sight:**
> "Negative contact. CONDOR 71."

**ATC with negative contact:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Traffic not in sight, turn right heading two-nine-zero for separation."

---

### 3.9 Airdrop Complete / Exit Romeo-4
**Co-Pilot reports:**
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Clear of Romeo-4. Airdrop complete. Requesting climb to flight level one-eight-zero and direct Iron Mountain."

**ATC approves:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Roger, clear of Romeo-4. Climb and maintain flight level one-eight-zero. Cleared direct Iron Mountain."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Climb and maintain flight level one-eight-zero, direct Iron Mountain. CONDOR 71."

---

### 3.10 Approach and Landing
**Co-Pilot requests:**
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Forty miles northeast. Request ILS approach, runway two-seven-left."

**ATC clears approach:**
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. Cleared ILS approach runway two-seven-left. Altimeter two-niner-niner-two. Contact Tower on one-one-eight-point-seven, ten miles final."

**Co-Pilot reads back:**
> "Cleared ILS runway two-seven-left, altimeter two-niner-niner-two, Tower one-one-eight-point-seven at ten miles. CONDOR 71."

## 4. EMERGENCY PHRASEOLOGY

### 4.1 MAYDAY Declaration (Co-Pilot transmits)
> "MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY. Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. [Emergency description]. [X] souls on board. [Fuel: X lbs]. [Position]. Request immediate [routing/approach/assistance]."

### 4.2 ATC Response to MAYDAY
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. MAYDAY acknowledged. Say souls on board and fuel remaining. Cleared direct [nearest airfield]. All traffic in your vicinity is being cleared. Report ready for approach."

### 4.3 PAN-PAN Declaration (Co-Pilot transmits)
> "PAN-PAN, PAN-PAN, PAN-PAN. Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. [Urgency description]. Request [assistance]."

### 4.4 ATC Response to PAN-PAN
> "CONDOR 71, Alpha Approach. PAN-PAN acknowledged. [Action]. Report status."

## 5. NON-STANDARD SITUATIONS

### 5.1 Requesting Clarification
If an ATC transmission is unclear or unexpected, the Co-Pilot does not readback until clarified:
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Say again / Confirm [specific item]."

### 5.2 Unable to Comply
If the Co-Pilot (on AC direction) cannot comply with an ATC instruction:
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Unable [instruction]. [Brief reason]. Request [alternative]."

ATC will never simply not respond — always acknowledge and provide an alternative or request clarification.

### 5.3 Lost Communications Protocol
Co-Pilot transmits blind on primary then guard:
> "Alpha Approach, CONDOR 71. Radio check." (×3 on 124.35)
> "All stations, CONDOR 71. NORDO [no radio]. Squawking seven-six-zero-zero. Proceeding [last cleared routing] to Iron Mountain." (on 121.5)
